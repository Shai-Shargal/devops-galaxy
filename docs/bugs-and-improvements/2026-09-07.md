## 🎯 What We Accomplished This Session

### 1. Galaxy Animation Refactor ✅
- **Changed from `spiral` to `starcluster`** animation type
- Starcluster rotates like a globe (right-to-left), simpler and more uniform
- Better suited for synchronization with service planets

### 2. Animation Synchronization ✅
- **Updated service planet rotation speed** to match galaxy
- `rotationSpeed: 0.0008 → 0.003` rad/frame (3.75x faster)
- Services now orbit in sync with the galaxy animation
- Creates a unified, cohesive visual experience

### 3. Key Insight Identified ✅
- **Root cause of previous "mismatch":** Galaxy spiral and service spiral were rotating at different speeds
- **Solution:** Simpler starcluster animation is easier to control and synchronize
- **Result:** No more disjointed animation feel
